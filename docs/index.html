        Pilot v1 / what stood out

        A useful baseline, not a verdict.

        01

        No observed label split

        Changing Coordinator / Resident to Guard / Prisoner did not change the structured authority choice in these matched episodes.

        02

        Order may matter

        All four cells investigated the weak allegation when it came first, and allowed or restored when it followed other dilemmas. Two orders cannot establish why.

        03

        Policy was easy to see

        The rulebook was explicit, and participants often cited the right rule before the authority decided. A harder follow-up should test that coaching separately.

        Pilot v1 / method & limits

        A small study with its workings open.

        One named model played both roles through fresh, isolated calls. Four dilemmas appeared in two matched orders under four role-and-governance conditions. A fixed software reviewer checked proposed restrictions in the review conditions; the primary score counted the proposal before that check.
